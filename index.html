About Us

We are Trivium Knightmares, a veteran FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) team located in Goodyear, AZ. As a team, we’re bringing students, parents, and mentors together to launch something exciting and impactful in our community.

FIRST Tech Challenge is a global middle and high school robotics program that combines the excitement of sport with the rigors of science and technology.

At Trivium Robotics Club, we are committed to providing students with hands-on STEM experiences that ignite curiosity and build future-ready skills.

Founded by a group of passionate friends and teachers who wanted to make STEM accessible for students at Trivium Preparatory Academy.
